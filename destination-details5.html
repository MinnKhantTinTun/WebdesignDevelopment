Offshore Industrial Views

Oil & Gas Platforms (Kinh Do Dau Khi)

Iconic offshore oil and gas platforms towering over 120m in Vung Tau Bay, showcasing Vietnam's modern energy industry from the sea.

Discover the Magic

The Oil & Gas Platforms (Kinh Do Dau Khi) represent Vietnam's key energy infrastructure in Vung Tau Bay. These massive structures over 120m tall dominate the offshore skyline, surrounded by support vessels, drilling rigs, and industrial activity — a striking symbol of modern technology and maritime engineering visible from high-speed canoe tours.

Must-Visit Attractions

Towering Drilling Rigs

Modern platforms exceeding 120m in height with advanced engineering.

Support & Supply Vessels

Busy fleet of ships servicing the offshore platforms.

Offshore Industrial Infrastructure

Complex network of pipes, towers, and energy facilities.

Panoramic Offshore Vistas

Striking sea skyline dominated by platforms and vessels.

Energy Industry Operations

Observe active oil/gas extraction from a safe distance.

Maritime & Industrial Activity

Dynamic scene of ships, cranes, and offshore work.
