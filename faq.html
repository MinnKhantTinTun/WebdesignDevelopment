What destinations do you cover?

We specialize in Vung Tau Bay tours, including Đảo Gò Găng (Oyster Kingdom), Làng Bè Long Sơn (floating village), Cụm cảng thương mại dịch vụ biển (sea commercial ports), giàn khoan dầu khí (oil platforms), and scenic western bay views.

Are your tours suitable for families and children?

Yes, most tours are family-friendly. Life jackets are provided for all ages, and short-duration tours are ideal for children. Please inform us in advance if traveling with kids under 12.

What should I bring on the tour?

Sunscreen, hat, sunglasses, comfortable clothes, towel, water bottle, camera/phone (waterproof pouch recommended), and motion sickness medicine if needed. Life jackets are provided.

How do I book a tour?

You can book directly through our website, by phone (0933.368.485), or via email (sales@marinatravel.com.vn). We recommend booking at least 1–2 days in advance, especially on weekends/holidays.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before departure. Cancellations within 24 hours or no-shows are non-refundable. Weather-related cancellations are fully refunded or rescheduled.

Can I change the tour date after booking?

Yes, subject to availability. Please contact us at least 24 hours in advance to reschedule.

What safety measures are in place?

All canoes are certified, equipped with life jackets, first-aid kits, and experienced captains. We monitor weather conditions daily and cancel/reschedule if unsafe.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, bank transfer, and popular mobile payments (Momo, ZaloPay). Full payment is required at booking or on the day (depending on arrangement).

Are there any age or health restrictions?

No strict age limit, but pregnant women, people with severe heart conditions or mobility issues should consult us first. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult.
