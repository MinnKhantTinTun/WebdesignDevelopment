Our Story

We Create Unique Bay Adventures,
Not Just Boat Rides.

Marina Travel (Công Ty TNHH Marinatravel) was established in 2021 by a team with extensive experience from various tourism companies, specializing in tour development and scenic sightseeing.

We focus on delivering safe, exciting speedboat explorations of Vung Tau Bay — from stunning sea views and floating fishing villages to commercial ports and offshore oil platforms. Our mission is to provide memorable, high-quality experiences on modern canoes while highlighting the unique beauty of southern Vietnam's coastline.
