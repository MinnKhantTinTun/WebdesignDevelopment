Sea Commercial Ports & Oil Platforms Tour

Vung Tau Bay, Vietnam 50-60 Minutes 4.8

About this Tour

Discover Vung Tau's maritime and industrial side from the sea. See major commercial ports, shipyards, cargo vessels, naval and coast guard ships, massive oil/gas platforms over 120m tall, and aquaculture villages. View Long Sơn Petrochemical Complex, PV OIL storage tanks, and bustling shipping activity at Gành Rái Bay. This short high-speed canoe tour offers a unique close-up of Vietnam's offshore industry and port operations.

Included
  • Round-trip high-speed canoe from Vung Tau
  • Life jackets & safety equipment
  • Guide commentary on industrial sites
  • Bottled water
Not Included
  • Hotel pickup/drop-off
  • Personal expenses & tips
  • Travel insurance
  • Meals/drinks
09:00

Check-in at Sao Mai Port

Arrive at Sao Mai Port (115B Trần Phú, Ward 5, Vung Tau), check-in, receive life jackets, group photos with sea view.

09:15

Departure & Port Exploration

Board high-speed canoe. Cruise past major ports: Đông Xuyên, Việt Xô, PTSC, large shipyards, naval/coast guard vessels, cargo ships at Gành Rái Bay, PV OIL tanks.

09:35

Oil & Gas Platforms

View modern drilling platforms over 120m tall and offshore oil/gas industry infrastructure.

09:55

Long Sơn Aquaculture Village

Observe oyster farms and traditional aquaculture from the sea.

10:10

Return & Arrival

Return via petrochemical complex views. Disembark at Sao Mai Port (tour ends 50-60 min total).
