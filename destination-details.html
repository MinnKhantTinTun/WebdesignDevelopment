Featured Island

Go Gang Island (Oyster Kingdom)

A pristine island paradise in Vung Tau Bay, famous for its oyster farms, serene beaches, mangrove forests, and fresh seafood experiences.

Discover the Magic

Go Gang Island offers a peaceful escape with untouched nature, traditional oyster cultivation, and the chance to enjoy authentic coastal life in Vung Tau Bay. Perfect for relaxation, eco-adventures, and savoring ultra-fresh seafood straight from the farms.

Must-Visit Attractions

Oyster Farms & Floating Rafts

Explore the famous oyster cultivation rafts and learn about local aquaculture traditions.

Secluded Beaches

Relax on quiet sandy shores with clear waters and gentle sea breezes.

Mangrove Forests

Walk through lush mangrove ecosystems teeming with birds and marine life.

Fresh Seafood Spots

Taste grilled oysters, squid, and other local catches right on the island.

Kayaking & Fishing

Paddle through calm waters or try recreational fishing in the bay.

Bird Watching Areas

Spot rare birds in the natural habitats around the island.
